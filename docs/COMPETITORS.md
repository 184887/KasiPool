## What makes KasiPool different

The existing platforms (Vuleka, Yebo Fresh) are **distributors**: they buy stock, warehouse it and resell it to shops. KasiPool is **not a shop in the middle**. It organises groups of businesses that buy directly from wholesalers, **with the money held in an account the group owns**. That means:
- **No warehouse and no stock risk** for KasiPool, so it's cheaper to start.
- **Owners keep control of their money**, which answers the trust problem from our interviews.
- **Businesses build buying power together**, like the foreign-owned networks do, instead of each shop depending on one supplier.

**One sentence for V1:** *Unlike stock platforms like Vuleka and Yebo Fresh, which sell to shops from their own warehouses, KasiPool helps businesses buy together straight from wholesalers, with the money kept in a bank account the group owns.*
