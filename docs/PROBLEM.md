PROBLEM:
Township businesses share common supply needs but operate in isolation, missing opportunities to reduce costs and improve profitability through economies of scale.

SOLUTION:
KasiPool helps small township businesses (spaza shops, taverns, salons, food sellers) buy stock together, so they get bulk prices without trusting anyone with their money. Nearby businesses order through WhatsApp, pay their share into a group bank account they own themselves, and get one bulk order delivered. Wholesalers pay KasiPool a small commission for bringing them a steady weekly order.

Local owners already buy together, but only informally, through handshakes and Facebook, and it all runs on personal trust. That keeps it small. KasiPool keeps the habit and adds the structure that's missing, while the money stays with the group. This saves owners both the bulk discount and the cost and time of trips to the cash and carry, and gives local businesses the buying power that organised foreign-owned networks already have.
