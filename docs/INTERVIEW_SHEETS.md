# KasiPool – Interview sheets

Print one sheet per interview. Keep it a conversation, not a quiz: you don't need every question. Ask before recording or taking photos.

---

## Sheet A: Spaza owner (Person 1)

**Date:** ______ **Area:** ______ **Interviewer:** ______
**Owner:** local / foreign-owned **Shop age:** ______ **OK to quote?** yes / no / anonymous
**Recorded?** yes / no

**Buying today**
1. Where do you buy your stock? How often do you go?
2. How do you get there and back? What does one trip cost (fare, extra for goods)?
3. Do you close the shop when you go? For how long?
4. Roughly how much do you spend on stock in a week or a month?
5. What are your top 5 products by how much you buy?

**Buying together**
6. Do you ever buy together with other shop owners? How does that work?
7. Has a group buy ever gone wrong? What happened? Who lost money?
8. Why do you think local owners buy together less than foreign-owned shops?

**Trust and payment (our biggest risk)**
9. Would you pay upfront in an app for cheaper stock delivered to your shop? Why / why not?
10. What would make you trust it? (Someone you know running it? Paying on delivery? A wholesaler's name behind it?)
11. How do you usually pay for stock: cash, EFT, card?
12. What delivery fee would be OK for you? R50? R100? R150?

**Close**
13. Would you try it if we ran a test in this area? Can we contact you? Number: ______

## Sheet B: Wholesaler / cash and carry (Person 2)

**Date:** ______ **Store:** ______ **Area:** ______ **Interviewer:** ______
**Person's role:** ______ **OK to quote?** yes / no / anonymous **Recorded?** yes / no

**Bulk buying**
1. When does someone count as a bulk customer? Is there a minimum order (value or pallets)?
2. How big is the bulk discount compared with normal price? (%)
3. Do you run specials? How do business customers find out (WhatsApp, leaflets)?

**Who buys**
4. Roughly what share of your bulk customers are local vs foreign-owned shops?
5. Do groups of shop owners buy together here? How do they organise it?

**Working with KasiPool**
6. If we brought you one pooled order from many local spazas, would you give it the bulk price?
7. Would you pay a commission for bringing new bulk customers? What % would be fair?
8. Do you deliver? To which areas, at what cost? What stops you delivering more (security, cost)?
9. Could our drivers collect from your depot?

**Close**
10. Who is the right person to talk to about a partnership? Contact: ______
