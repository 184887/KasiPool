What one field visit told us

Our first field visit changed our assumptions

We assumedWe found
Local shop owners compete too much to cooperateEarly signs say otherwise: owners will buy together when it pays
Group buying would be a new habitIt exists, informally: handshakes, Facebook groups, personal trust
Bulk buying gives a big savingModest: about 2.5–5% on price, per one wholesaler
Wholesalers would resist a middlemanOne wholesaler was positive, but wouldn’t share a price list

Source: conversations with shop owners and one hybrid wholesaler, 21 Oct 2026.

3

We spoke to people outside a cash and carry and to staff at one hybrid wholesale/retail store. These are early signals from a small sample, and we say so. The saving is modest, which is why transport and time matter in the value story. The 2.5–5% figure comes from one wholesaler and still needs rechecking against the recording.
