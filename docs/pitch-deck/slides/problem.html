Township shops buy the same stock, but each buys alone

1

Too small for bulk prices

At one wholesaler, bulk prices start at a full pallet. A spaza needs one or two units of a product.

2

Costly trips to restock

Small, frequent trips to the cash and carry make transport costly per unit of stock.

3

Bigger buying groups get the better price

Groups of about 10–15 shops already buy together and get the bulk price. Most local shop owners buy alone.

Sources: one wholesaler interview, 21 Oct 2026; Standard Bank Township Informal Economy Report, Oct 2025, pp. 8, 13

2

Spaza shops share the same supply needs, the same staples from the same wholesalers, but they buy in isolation. Standard Bank’s 2025 research names the absence of group buying benefits and transport difficulties as common challenges.
