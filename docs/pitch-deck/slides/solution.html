KasiPool lists offers on a simple mobile web app, each with a deadline

1

Offer goes live

Product, bulk price, target and deadline, all shown up front

2

Shops choose

Each shop owner picks how many cases; the app adds up the pool

3

Pay to confirm

Only paid orders count toward the supplier minimum

4

Buy or refund

Target met: one bulk order. Not met: everyone is refunded

5

Delivered or collected

A trusted packing partner sorts each shop’s cases

WhatsApp supports sharing, reminders and help. Shop owners can suggest products for future offers.

5

Interest alone doesn’t secure stock. Shop owners pay through a third-party payment gateway. If paid orders don’t reach the supplier minimum by the deadline, the offer is cancelled and payments are refunded under a disclosed process.
