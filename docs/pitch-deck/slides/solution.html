KasiPool runs timed group-buy offers on a simple mobile web app

1

Offer goes live

Product, pooled price, target and deadline, all shown up front

2

Traders choose

Each trader picks a quantity; the app combines demand

3

Pay to confirm

Only paid orders count toward the supplier minimum

4

Buy or refund

Target met: one bulk order. Not met: everyone is refunded

5

Sorted and delivered

A vetted partner splits the order into each trader’s share

WhatsApp supports sharing, reminders and help. Traders can suggest products for future offers.

5

Interest alone doesn’t secure stock. Traders pay through a third-party payment gateway. If paid demand doesn’t reach the supplier minimum by the deadline, the offer is cancelled and payments are refunded under a disclosed process.
