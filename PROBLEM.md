PROBLEM:
Township businesses share common supply needs but operate in isolation, missing opportunities to reduce costs and improve profitability through economies of scale.

SOLUTION:
KasiPool is an app that lets local spaza owners pool their orders into bulk purchases, so they get the same wholesale prices that organised foreign-owned networks already get. Owners reserve the staples they need within a short campaign window and pay upfront through the app. KasiPool combines the orders into one bulk order with a partner wholesaler and has it delivered directly to each shop. Delivery is confirmed by scanning a QR code.

Local owners already buy together, but only informally, through handshakes and Facebook, and it all runs on personal trust. KasiPool keeps the habit and adds the structure that's missing: payments held safely, verified orders, confirmed delivery and an in-app way to sort out problems. This saves owners both the bulk discount and the cost and time of trips to the cash and carry. It also brings wholesalers a new group of local customers they currently don't reach.
